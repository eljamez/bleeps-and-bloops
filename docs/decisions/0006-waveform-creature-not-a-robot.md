# 0006: A waveform creature, not a robot

- **Status:** accepted
- **Note:** [0010](0010-bleeps-runs-the-crew-bloops-reflects.md) keeps this mark. The word Bloops is the agent, not the round bump.
- **Date:** 2026-11-12

## Context

Bleeps and Bloops needed a mark. The obvious move for an AI dev tool is a robot mascot. Robot mascots are already the default for this kind of product, and they say nothing about what this system is. A bleep is a short spike. A bloop is the round result that comes out.

## Decision

The mark is one stroke: a flat line, a small triangular bleep, then a round bloop with two eyes, so the bump reads as a little creature peeking up. Not a robot, not an antenna, not a face with a mouth.

Files, colors, and usage are in [brand](../brand.md).

## Consequences

- New artwork starts from this mark. A later pass does not invent a robot to replace it.
- The wordmark is Nunito Bold, with the ampersand in the brand orange.
- **Revisit if:** James picks a different mark, or the eyes stop reading at the sizes we actually use.
